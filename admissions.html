Your Next Step

Every Great Journey Begins With a First Step.

Choosing a school is an important decision for every family. At Ashraf Islamia Model Public School, we aim to create an environment where students can build strong academic foundations while developing discipline, confidence, character, and a lifelong desire to learn.

Explore the information below to understand the general admission journey and the next steps for your family.

Why Begin Your Journey With Us?

An education should prepare students not only for the next examination, but for the opportunities and responsibilities that follow.

01

Academic Foundation

Encouraging strong learning habits, understanding, curiosity, and academic growth.

02

Character & Discipline

Helping students develop responsibility, integrity, respect, and self-discipline.

03

Student Development

Supporting confidence, communication, teamwork, creativity, and personal growth.

04

A Supportive Community

Creating an environment where students, educators, and families can grow together.

Who Can Apply?

Admission availability and eligibility may vary depending on grade level and available places.

Early / Primary Levels

Students entering the school's early and primary learning stages may be considered according to the school's current admission criteria.

Middle Levels

Students seeking admission at middle-school levels may be considered subject to academic requirements and available places.

Secondary Levels

Admission at secondary levels may involve additional academic considerations depending on the grade and current school policy.

Please contact the school administration for the latest eligibility and availability information.

How Admission Works

A simple overview of the typical admission journey.

01

Enquire

Contact the school or visit the campus to learn about current admission availability and requirements.

02

Explore

Discuss the appropriate grade level, academic expectations, and relevant information with the school administration.

03

Submit

Provide the required information and documents according to the school's current admission procedure.

04

Review

The school reviews the submitted information and follows its applicable admission process.

05

Confirm

Once the admission process is completed, the school provides the next instructions to the family.

Documents You May Need

Required documents can vary depending on grade level and the school's current admission policy. Please confirm the latest requirements with the school office.

  • Student identification / registration document
  • Parent or guardian identification
  • Previous academic record
  • Recent photographs
  • Transfer-related documents, where applicable
  • Other documents requested by the school
Please confirm the current document requirements with the school administration before submitting an application.

Before You Apply

Essential guidelines and considerations for prospective families.

Availability

Admission is subject to available places in the relevant grade.

Eligibility

Requirements may differ according to the student's grade level and academic background.

Schedule

Admission dates and timelines are subject to the school's current academic calendar.

Contact

For the latest information, families should contact the school administration directly.

Fee information is available from the school administration and may vary according to grade level and applicable school policies.
